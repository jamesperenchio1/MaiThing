Save money. Save food. Help the planet.

MaiThing is a surplus-food marketplace for Thailand. Cafés, bakeries, and restaurants list their unsold food at a steep discount — you reserve it in the app and pick it up yourself, before it goes to waste.

How it works

  • Browse nearby. Find surprise bags and fixed deals from shops around you.
  • Reserve in app. Get a pickup code for your order — quantities are limited.
  • Pick up & enjoy. Show your code during the pickup window and rescue great food at a great price.

For food businesses

MaiThing gives merchants a simple way to turn surplus inventory into revenue instead of waste, with tools for listings, pickup windows, promotions, and payouts. Reach out via the support page to learn about partnering with us.
