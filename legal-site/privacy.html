Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

MaiThing ("we", "us") is operated by James Perenchio, a sole proprietor based in Thailand. This policy explains what personal data the MaiThing app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at jamyangperenchio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account, and whether you're using MaiThing as a customer or a food-business merchant.
  • Location. With your permission, your approximate or precise device location, used to show nearby merchants and listings on the map and in search.
  • Camera and photos. With your permission, camera access to scan pickup QR codes (customers) or verify pickups (merchants), and photo-library access for merchants to upload listing photos.
  • Order and wallet activity. Listings you reserve, orders you place, pickup history, reviews you leave, and your in-app wallet balance and transaction history.
  • Push notification tokens. A device push token so we can send you order and pickup updates, which you can disable in your device settings or in-app notification preferences at any time.
  • Merchant business information. For merchant accounts: business name, address, opening hours, bank account details for payouts, and staff you invite to your team.
  • Usage and device data. Basic technical information such as device type and app version, used for debugging and improving the app.

How we use your information

  • To operate your account and process your orders and reservations.
  • To show you relevant nearby merchants and listings.
  • To send order, pickup, and account-related notifications.
  • To pay out merchant earnings and manage merchant tools.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the app.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not currently use your data for third-party advertising.

Payment information

MaiThing includes an in-app wallet used for order payments. If and when we enable real-money top-ups through a third-party payment processor, that processor will handle your card details directly — MaiThing does not store your full card number.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase, our backend and database provider, which stores your account and order data on our behalf.
  • Google Maps, to display merchant locations and provide directions.
  • Expo, to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Food-business merchants you order from, who receive the order and pickup details needed to fulfill your order.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as needed to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations, or to resolve disputes.

Your choices

  • You can review and update your profile information in the app's Profile section.
  • You can turn off location, camera, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings; some features (such as nearby search or QR pickup) won't work without them.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing jamyangperenchio@gmail.com, subject to data we're required to retain for legal or accounting reasons.

Children's privacy

MaiThing is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
