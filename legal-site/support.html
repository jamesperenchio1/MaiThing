Support

Need help with an order, a pickup, your wallet, or your merchant account? We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email jamyangperenchio@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your account email and, if it's about an order, the order or pickup code.

Common questions

I didn't get my pickup code

Pickup codes appear on your order detail screen once a reservation is confirmed. If you don't see one, check your connection and pull to refresh — if it's still missing after that, email us with your order details.

I need to cancel or get a refund

You can cancel an order from the order detail screen before the pickup window begins. For issues after pickup, or anything the app doesn't let you resolve yourself, contact us directly.

I'm a merchant and want to join MaiThing

Email us at the address above with your business name, location, and what you'd like to list — we'll walk you through onboarding.

I want to delete my account or my data

Email us with your account's email address and we will delete your account and associated personal data, subject to what we're required to keep for legal or accounting reasons. See our Privacy Policy for details.
